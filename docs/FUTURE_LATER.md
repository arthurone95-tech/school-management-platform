# School Management Platform — Deferred & Future Features (FUTURE_LATER.md)

**Status:** Deferred / Post-Launch Roadmap.  
**Note:** These features are explicitly put on hold to keep the initial deployment light, fast, and easy for schools to test.

---

## Deferred Items

1. **Online Fee Collection & Payment Gateway:**
   - Online mobile money / card fee payments through the app.
   - *(Note: Fee balance calculations on report cards are included in `AGENT_NOW.md`).*
2. **SMS & Automated Messaging:**
   - SMS notification gateway, broadcast messages, exam alerts, fee reminders.
3. **HR & Staff Payroll:**
   - Staff salary slips, tax calculations, contract tracking, payroll reports.
4. **Transportation Management:**
   - Bus routes, vehicle management, transport fees. *(Ruled out / Not needed).*
5. **Library Management:**
   - Book cataloging, borrowing histories, barcodes, library fines.
6. **Advanced LMS & Homework Uploads:**
   - Online homework submissions, lesson plan tracking, digital learning materials.
7. **Parent Account Management:**
   - Dedicated parent accounts separate from student portal credentials. *(Using unified student/parent portal for launch).*
8. **Complex Security & Audit Tools:**
   - Automated IP restrictions, advanced threat detection, session revocation consoles.
